## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**That's Not My Neighbor** is a captivating social deduction game for PC that combines simulation and management elements. As a doorman tasked with stopping doppelgangers from entering your neighbors' apartments, you'll find yourself making critical decisions that affect the lives of those around you. The **latest version** offers a complete experience with all features available, ensuring you have the best gameplay possible. 

Immerse yourself in a world of tension and decision-making where you must scrutinize documents and behaviors to keep your neighbors safe. Download the **full version** today and test your skills in this thrilling simulation game.
